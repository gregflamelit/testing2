THE EXPERIMENT

One split. No shortcuts.

Exact duplicate chemistry profiles stay in the same split. A fixed random forest learns only from the training partition. A separate calibration partition sets the prediction interval. The test partition is used for reporting, not fitting.

No scaling or imputation is used: the supplied numeric dataset is complete. The quality column is excluded from all eleven inputs. Seed 42; calibration seed 43.

96 trees · maximum depth 10 · minimum 3 samples per leaf · 80% feature sampling. Fixed settings; no test-set tuning.

WHICH FEATURES HELP?

WHERE IT GETS THINGS WRONG

The edges are harder.

Observed qualityTest samplesMAE

Rare quality scores have small sample counts. A strong average result can conceal errors on exceptional or poor wines.

READ BEFORE POURING

Confidence needs context.

  • Quality is a subjective sensory score, not a safety assessment.
  • This dataset represents Portuguese red Vinho Verde; results may not transfer to other regions, vintages, or wine styles.
  • Changing one chemical attribute does not simulate a real production intervention. Input combinations may be unrealistic even within individual ranges.
  • The interval assumes future data are exchangeable with calibration data. Distribution shifts can reduce coverage.
  • Use the forecast to prioritize sensory review. It cannot replace tasting or laboratory testing.
